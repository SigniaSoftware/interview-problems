# Engineering take-home: stop-loss claims monitor

Thank you for taking the time to do this assessment.

You'll build a small full-stack app for a fictional health insurer that sells stop-loss cover to self-funded
employers. Analysts upload monthly claim files, the app tracks each member's spend for the policy year, and it raises
alerts as members get close to their deductible. No insurance background is needed.

We expect you to use AI tools (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, agents, whatever you work with), and we're interested in
how you use them. The core should take about 10–12 hours with AI tools.

## Getting started

Clone or download this repository and build your solution in **your own new repository**. There's no need to fork
this one, and please don't open pull requests or issues against it.

Some rules are less precise than they could be, as in real work. Make a sensible call and record it in
your `DECISIONS.md`; the brief explains what we're looking for.

## Submitting

Submit your work as a **public GitHub repository** and send us the link by the deadline in your invitation. Keep
your real commit history; please don't squash it. The [Submit](BRIEF.md#submit) section of the brief lists the
documents to include.

If anything is unclear about the process itself, reply to your invitation email.

All data here is synthetic. Nothing you build will be used in our product.
